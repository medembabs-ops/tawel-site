About

The house was built around a signature before it was built around a collection.

Origin

Tawel Style began with a single piece of handwriting — a name, written once, in Bordeaux ink, and never redrawn. Everything the house makes since has been an attempt to earn a place next to that mark: cut with the same restraint, finished with the same patience.

Four colours. One hand. No second script.
